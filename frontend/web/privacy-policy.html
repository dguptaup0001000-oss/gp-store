Privacy Policy — GP-Store

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This page describes what the GP-Store software actually collects and does. It is not legal advice. A lawyer familiar with Indian data protection and e-commerce rules (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act) should review it before a Play Store listing is treated as final.

Who we are

GP-Store is a grocery shop. The customer app is in.gpstore.customer. Store staff use a separate admin app, in.gpstore.admin. Delivery workers use a third app, com.gpstore.worker. The production API is https://api.gpstore.co.in/v1.

Information we collect

  • Name, mobile number, and optionally email, when you create an account.
  • Delivery addresses. If you tap “use my current location” while adding an address, the app reads GPS once to fill the pin. Location is not tracked in the background.
  • Order history, including items, amounts, and delivery status.
  • Chosen payment method (Cash on Delivery, UPI, or online checkout when configured). We do not store card numbers, bank passwords, or UPI PINs. Online payments, when enabled, are processed by Cashfree.
  • Reviews and ratings you submit.
  • A device push token if Firebase Cloud Messaging is configured, used only for order and account messages.
  • Optional voice search uses the on-device speech recogniser. Audio is not stored or uploaded by GP-Store.

Delivery workers and delivery partners

Packing staff use a separate app, com.gpstore.worker. That app may read location only while a delivery screen is open (foreground). There is no background location permission. Tracking stops when the worker leaves that screen or signs out. Camera is used only to scan the QR code on a packed order label.

The customer app (in.gpstore.customer) does not include a delivery dashboard and does not use the camera. Packing staff use the worker app above.

Store administration

Shop administrators use the admin app (in.gpstore.admin) and may connect a paired Bluetooth thermal printer to print receipts. Bluetooth is not used to scan for people or for advertising. The customer app does not include store-management screens.

How we use information

  • To fulfil and deliver orders inside configured delivery territories.
  • To send order-status updates.
  • To personalise recommendations from your own order history.
  • To respond to support requests.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information to third parties.
  • We do not share data with advertisers.
  • We do not request background location.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete saved addresses in the app.
  • Delete your own reviews.
  • Delete your account in Profile → Delete Account. That requires your current password (or a password you set first) so a stolen session alone cannot destroy the account. You can also request deletion on the web.

Contact

Use Contact Us in the app, or the details on the account deletion page.
