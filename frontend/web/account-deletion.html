Request Account Deletion - GP-Store

This page lets you request deletion of your GP-Store account and personal data without needing the app installed, as required by Google Play's account deletion policy.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

If you still have the GP-Store app installed: open it, go to Profile → Delete Account, and confirm. This takes effect immediately - no waiting on a request.

Option 2: Request deletion from here

Email support with the phone number or email address your account uses, and the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We'll delete it within a reasonable timeframe and confirm once done.

What actually gets deleted

Your name, phone number, email, saved addresses, wishlist, and cart are permanently removed. You're signed out of every device immediately.

Your past order and invoice history is retained (shown as "Deleted User" going forward) rather than deleted outright - Indian tax law requires keeping invoice records for a retention period regardless of account deletion requests. It will no longer be linked to your name, phone, or email once your account is deleted.
